## EU

### CSRD after Omnibus I
- **Directive (EU) 2026/470 (Omnibus I)** was published on 26 Feb 2026 and entered into force on 18 Mar 2026.
- **Scope:** EU undertakings (and EU parent groups) exceeding **both** €450m net turnover and an average of 1,000 employees, for FYs beginning on or after **1 Jan 2027** (first reports in 2028).
- **Non-EU parents:** EU net turnover above €450m in each of the last two FYs **and** an EU subsidiary or branch with net turnover above €200m — reporting for FYs beginning on or after **1 Jan 2028** (reports in 2029).
- **Wave 1 companies** now below the thresholds: Member States may exempt them for FY2025–FY2026; otherwise reporting may continue until FY2027 rules apply. Check the relevant Member State's transposition.
- **Transposition deadline** for the reporting articles: **19 Mar 2027**.
- Sources: [Weil, Mar 2026](https://www.weil.com/-/media/files/pdfs/2026/march/the-omnibus-i-directive-now-in-force.pdf); [Grant Thornton, May 2026](https://www.grantthornton.com/content/dam/grantthornton/website/assets/content-page-files/audit/pdfs/2026/snapshot-2026-05-impact-of-final-changes-to-european-sustainability-reporting-requirements.pdf); [Crowell & Moring](https://www.crowell.com/en/insights/client-alerts/eu-sustainability-reporting-revamp-key-updates-to-the-csrd-and-the-cs3d-from-the-omnibus-i-directive); [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2026/germany/omnibus-i-directive-entered-into-force-%E2%80%93-a-first-overview-of-the-amended-csrd-and-csddd).

### Revised (simplified) ESRS
- Commission adopted the revised ESRS on 3 Jul 2026; the scrutiny period ended on 4 Sep 2026 without objection; published as **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563** in the Official Journal on 21 Sep 2026; **enters into force 10 Nov 2026**; mandatory for FYs beginning on or after **1 Jan 2027**, with early application permitted for FY2026.
- Key changes: mandatory datapoints reduced by 61% (per the Commission's text describing EFRAG's revision); a more proportionate DMA (top-down or bottom-up; no need to assess every possible IRO; reasonable and supportable information without undue cost or effort); new reliefs and phase-ins, including for anticipated financial effects; improved interoperability with ISSB Standards.
- FY2026 options described in public summaries: continue with ESRS Set 1, early-adopt the revised ESRS, or a hybrid with selected reliefs.
- **Digital tagging:** EFRAG published a draft XBRL taxonomy for the revised ESRS on 17 Sep 2026 (consultation to 11 Nov 2026). Timing of mandatory tagging remains unsettled (*uncertain*).
- Sources: [EUR-Lex 2026/1563](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2026/1563/oj/eng); [EUR-Lex C(2026)5010](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=intcom%3AC%282026%295010); [Linklaters](https://sustainablefutures.linklaters.com/post/102n88h/eu-csrd-commission-adopts-revised-esrs-and-voluntary-reporting-standard); [PwC Viewpoint deep dive](https://viewpoint.pwc.com/gx/en/pwc/in-depth/2026/id_int202603.html); [EFRAG taxonomy consultation](https://www.efrag.org/en/projects/esrs-xbrl-taxonomy/exposure-draft-consultation).

### EU Taxonomy
- **Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/73** simplifies reporting; applies from 1 Jan 2026 (FY2025 reports), with the option to apply the previous rules in full for FY2025. Introduces a 10% materiality threshold for non-financial undertakings' activity assessment and allows OpEx omission where not material.
- Sources: [EUR-Lex 2026/73](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32026R0073); [BDO](https://www.bdo.global/en-gb/news/ifrs-news/eu-publishes-delegated-act-introducing-simplified-eu-taxonomy-requirements).

### United Kingdom
- **UK SRS S1 and S2** (UK-endorsed ISSB Standards) published 25 Feb 2026; available for voluntary use.
- **FCA PS26/19** (30 Sep 2026): listed issuers report against UK SRS on a **comply-or-explain** basis for accounting periods beginning on or after **1 Jan 2027** (first reports 2028); transitional relief of 1 year for Scope 3 and 2 years for UK SRS S1 disclosures. Replaces the TCFD-based listing rule.
- Sources: [GOV.UK UK SRS](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-sustainability-reporting-standards-uk-srs-s1-and-uk-srs-s2); [FCA PS26/19](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/policy-statements/ps26-19-aligning-listed-issuers-sustainability-disclosures-international-standards).

## United States

### California
- **SB 253:** first report (prior fiscal-year Scope 1 and 2) due **10 Nov 2026**, extended from 10 Aug 2026 under CARB's revised Initial Regulation, resubmitted to the Office of Administrative Law on 21 Sep 2026 and **pending approval** when checked. CARB guidance indicates limited assurance is not required in the first year and describes first-year enforcement discretion (including for entities not collecting data as of 5 Dec 2024). Scope 3 reporting begins in 2027. Sources: [CARB guidance (Sep 2026)](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/sites/default/files/2026-09/2026_SB253_Reporting_Guidance.pdf); [Ropes & Gray](https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/viewpoints/2026/09/102o3aw/california-sb-253-261-update-carbs-2026-initial-regulation-nears-the-finish-li); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/california-climate-disclosure-laws-carb-releases-optional-intake-platform-and); [BDO](https://www.bdo.com/insights/sustainability-and-esg/california-issues-guidance-for-2026-sb-253-ghg-reporting-submissions).
- **SB 261:** enforcement **enjoined** by the Ninth Circuit on 18 Nov 2025 pending appeal; the court declined to enjoin SB 253. Oral argument was heard in January 2026; **no merits ruling reported as of early Oct 2026** (*moderate confidence — check the docket*). Sources: [Jones Day](https://www.jonesday.com/en/insights/2025/11/ninth-circuit-enjoins-sb-261s-climaterelated-risk-reporting-requirements-declines-to-enjoin-sb-253); [Foley & Lardner](https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/01/ninth-circuit-hears-oral-argument-in-challenge-to-california-climate-disclosure-l/); [White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/california-climate-disclosure-laws-ninth-circuit-hears-oral-argument-no-ruling-yet).

### SEC climate disclosure rule
- The 2024 rules were **stayed** by the SEC (Apr 2024) and **never took effect**. The SEC withdrew its defence (Mar 2025); the Eighth Circuit has held the case in **abeyance** since 12 Sep 2025 pending SEC reconsideration through notice-and-comment. The SEC published a **proposed rescission** (Federal Register, 3 Jun 2026; release 33-11421). On 21 May 2026 the court denied a motion to lift the abeyance. A final rescission had not been reported as of the check date (*uncertain — verify*).
- Sources: [Federal Register, 3 Jun 2026](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/06/03/2026-11091/rescission-of-climate-related-disclosure-rules); [SEC proposed rule 33-11421](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/proposed/2026/33-11421.pdf); [InsideEPA, May 2026](https://insideepa.com/climate-blog/8th-circuit-rejects-call-restart-case-over-sec-climate-rules).
