:::danger[Consult your independence team]
Independence rules are jurisdiction- and firm-specific. This page lists general considerations to prompt the right conversation. It is not a substitute for your firm's independence policies or a consultation.
:::

## Relevant frameworks (as of Oct 2026)

- **IESBA International Ethics Standards for Sustainability Assurance (IESSA)**, including International Independence Standards — effective for sustainability assurance engagements on information for periods beginning on or after **15 Dec 2026** (with a later date of 1 Jul 2028 available for certain value-chain-component provisions); early adoption permitted. Source: [IESBA](https://www.ethicsboard.org/focus-areas/global-ethics-sustainability-standards).
- Local regulations and professional body rules (for example SEC/PCAOB rules for issuer audit clients, EU audit regulation where applicable, national requirements under CSRD transposition).
- Your firm's own policies, which are often stricter.

## Two different questions

| Question | Examples | Key considerations |
|---|---|---|
| **Using AI in our assurance work** | Analytics, document matching, drafting working papers | Tool approved for assurance use; client data handling; documentation; the team still performs and evidences the procedures |
| **Providing AI-enabled services to an assurance client** | Building their inventory, extraction pipeline, evidence pack, controls, or licensing them a tool | Self-review threat (assuring something we built), management responsibility threat (making their decisions), and prohibited non-assurance services under applicable rules |

## Checklist before starting

- [ ] Is the entity (or an affiliate) an audit or sustainability assurance client, or likely to become one?
- [ ] Would the service create information, systems or controls we would later assure?
- [ ] Would we be making management decisions (materiality, methodology choices, approving data)?
- [ ] Does any automation we provide perform a function that management must own?
- [ ] Are fees, licensing or business relationships involved (for example, the client licensing a firm tool)?
- [ ] Has the independence team been consulted and the outcome documented?

## Safer patterns (subject to consultation)

- Readiness **assessments** that point out gaps, rather than building the client's processes.
- Training the client's team on general practices, without making their decisions.
- Assurance teams using AI internally, with approved tools and full documentation, without supplying the outputs as the client's own records.
