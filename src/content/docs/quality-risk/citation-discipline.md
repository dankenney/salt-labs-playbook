## In deliverables

- **Cite to the smallest useful unit:** document, version/date, page and paragraph or table row. "ESRS" is not a citation; "Delegated Regulation (EU) 2026/1563, ESRS E1, para X" is.
- **Primary over secondary.** Regulations, standards and official guidance first. Commentary (law firms, accounting firms, vendors) is secondary and should be labelled as such.
- **Quote, then paraphrase.** For critical requirements, include the exact quote alongside your interpretation.
- **Verify every AI citation** you keep. Open it. If you cannot find the text, delete the claim or find a real source.
- **Date your regulatory statements.** "As of 7 Oct 2026, …" Regulatory facts expire.
- **No citation, no claim.** Unsupported statements are removed, not softened.

## On this site

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Every regulatory statement links a source | Prefer the official source; add a reputable secondary summary if helpful |
| Status rows carry a checked date | In the [regulatory tracker](/reference/regulatory-tracker/) |
| Uncertainty is stated | "No ruling reported as of…", "pending approval", "moderate confidence" |
| No vendor claims without a link | The [tools landscape](/tools/landscape/) cites vendors' own public pages and says what we have not verified |
| No invented statistics | Time savings are labelled estimates with assumptions |
| No internal or client information | This site uses public sources only |

## Citation format

`Publisher, *Title* (date or version), location. URL`

Example: IAASB, *ISSA 5000, General Requirements for Sustainability Assurance Engagements* (Nov 2024), para 15 (effective date). https://www.iaasb.org/publications/international-standard-sustainability-assurance-5000-general-requirements-sustainability-assurance
